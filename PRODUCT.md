# DwellPlay

## Register

product

## Users

People watching their own phone videos, YouTube links, Jellyfin libraries and stream links on a phone or on a parked car screen. The first real-car target is Gaurav's Tata Curvv with projected Android Auto. The same app also serves cars running Android Automotive OS.

## Product Purpose

Bring a person's media together in one adaptive player. Make choosing a video and continuing it simple. Let eligible native media continue as audio when the picture must disappear. Require a deliberate action to restore video after a drive.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, restrained, deliberate. Native Android interaction with quiet depth and Algothrim crimson as a precise identity signal. Content leads the interface.

## Anti-references

Generic teal developer-player chrome, an iOS clone, decorative glass on every card, glossy refractive bubbles, dense automotive controls and continuously moving decoration. The user supplied the Dark Cinema direction and approved the Home/Player refinement on 1 October 2026.

## Design Principles

- Media first, controls second, brand third.
- Glass for controls; artwork for atmosphere; crimson for intent.
- Recompose for the window rather than squeezing one layout into every display.
- Keep navigation consistent across phone and car, with four primary destinations.
- Preserve the tested platform behavior and visible parked/resume states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable neutral text rather than small crimson text. Pair color with labels and icons. Retain large car targets and typography, visible focus/selection, Android system font scaling and system motion preferences. YouTube playback chrome stays outside the active official player. Automotive restrictions fully hide the app's picture; translucent surfaces never weaken that cover.
